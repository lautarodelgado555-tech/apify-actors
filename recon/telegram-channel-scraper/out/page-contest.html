Deadline: 23:59 Dubai time @ May 17, 2026
Who can participate: only those who secured 🥈2nd or 🥉3rd places in Round 1.

📱 Telegram announces an express round of the Design Contest for selected winners of our previous competition.

The Task:
Identify weak spots in existing features of our official apps (Android, iOS, Telegram Desktop) and propose improvements – fixing the flaws and making the target features more elegant, convenient, simple, clear, or even more modern.

Contest Requirements:

1. You may submit up to 2 features.

2. Submissions must be in Before / After format.

4. Anything is within the scope – copy, spacing, colors, visuals – as long as the change noticeably improves the user experience.

Standout submissions will receive additional reward of up to $1,000 per each feature you improve. The best of you may get a chance to join the Telegram Team.

🏆 Digital Freedom Contest: Clarifications and Submissions

We are glad to provide clarifications regarding the numerous similar questions we received:

Q: How many videos can be submitted?
A: You can submit multiple videos across different platforms – e.g., 3 separate videos for X and 2 for TikTok – or one video for each qualifying platform (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat or X), provided each separate video gained at least 10,000 views. Videos that gained fewer than 10,000 views will not be accepted for the competition and will not be eligible for a share of the prize fund.

Note: Videos we deem low-quality or suspect of having generated non-organic views will be excluded.


Q: When will submissions open, and how can I send my video(s)?
A: We will post a separate message in this channel in the coming days, once @ContestBot begins accepting submissions. You will have at least several days to submit your work, so do not worry – you will not miss it.

Note: @ContestBot is the only tool you will use to submit your work.


Q: I'm worried that my video will be stolen and submitted by someone else.
A: You must include your Telegram @username in the caption of the video on the external platform. This username must match the @username of the account from which you will submit the video. Videos that does not have a proper @username caption, will be discarded from the evaluation.
